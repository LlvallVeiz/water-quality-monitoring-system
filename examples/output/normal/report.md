## Notes

- NSF WQI here is a six-parameter NSF-style index (DO, pH, nitrate, turbidity, TDS, temperature change) with weights renormalised over the parameters present, per the BASIN subset rule.
- CCME WQI uses PP 22/2021 class II objectives (pH 6-9, DO >= 4 mg/L, nitrate <= 10 mg/L as N, TDS <= 1000 mg/L) plus a project operational turbidity objective of 50 NTU; the WHO 5 NTU target applies to treated drinking water, not a raw river.
- Temperature exceedance is measured as deviation from the station median temperature (PP 22/2021 limits deviation, not absolute temperature).
